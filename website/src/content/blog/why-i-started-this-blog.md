# Why I Started This Blog

I'm studying Applied Mathematics–Computer Science
at Brown University, with interests in machine
learning, computer systems, and security.

I created this website to document what I'm learning
and share my thoughts on technical subjects.

## What I'll Write About

### Research Paper Reviews

I plan to analyze research papers on machine learning,
LLMs, security, and computer systems.

My goal is to go beyond summarizing papers and
examine their methods, results, and limitations.

### Technical Projects

I'll document my programming projects and experiments,
including the challenges I encounter and what I learn.

### Books and Concepts

I'll also share notes from technical books and explore
computer science concepts that interest me.

## Goals

I hope this website becomes a useful collection
of my ideas, experiments, and learning experiences.
